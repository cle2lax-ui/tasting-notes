Tasting Notes

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 20, 2026

Tasting Notes is a personal wine-tasting notebook shared privately among friends. It has no servers, no accounts of its own, no advertising and no analytics. This page explains exactly what happens to your notes.

Notes stay on your device

Every tasting note and label photo you enter is saved in your browser's storage on your own iPhone, iPad or computer. Nothing is sent to the person who made the app or to anyone else.

Optional: Google Sheets

If you choose to connect Google, the app asks Google for one limited permission ("See, edit, create and delete only the specific Google Drive files you use with this app"). With it, the app can:

  • create a Google Sheet called “Blind Tasting Notes” in your own Google Drive, or open the one Sheet you choose in Google's file picker;
  • read and write your tasting notes in that Sheet's “Tasting Log” tab;
  • save label photos you add to a folder it creates in your Drive called “Blind Tasting Notes — Labels”.

The app cannot see any other files in your Google Drive, your email, your contacts or anything else in your Google account. The connection goes directly from your device to Google; your notes never pass through any other server. The sign-in token Google issues is kept only on your device and expires on its own. The app also keeps the email address of the Google account you connected, on your device only, so Google can reconnect you without asking which account to use.

You can disconnect at any time in the app (Settings › Google Sheet › Disconnect) and remove the app's access in your Google Account under Security › Third-party apps with account access. Your Sheet and photos stay in your Drive until you delete them.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Backups and sharing

Backups, spreadsheet downloads and shared flights are files you create and send yourself, through your device's share sheet. The app does not send them anywhere on its own.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app from your Home Screen, or clearing this website's data in your browser settings, removes the notes stored on that device. Delete the Google Sheet and photo folder in Google Drive to remove those copies.

Questions

Ask the friend who shared the app with you, or visit the app's page at github.com/cle2lax-ui/tasting-notes.
