# Blind Tasting Notes

A home-screen app for recording blind tasting notes with a systematic approach to tasting:
appearance, nose, structure and palate, quality and aging, then your guess against the reveal.

**Open it:** https://cle2lax-ui.github.io/tasting-notes/ — on iPhone or iPad tap Share › Add to Home Screen.

- Works offline; notes are saved on your own device.
- Optional: connect a Google Sheet in your own Google Drive (Settings › Google Sheet) to keep a copy and sync between devices.
- Back up, restore, download for Excel, and share a flight of wines with friends.

For personal study. Adapted from the WSET Level 4 SAT and Wine-Lexicon.
